Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my ships and shields on a new phone?

Open the Hangar, choose Store and tap Restore Purchases. Ships, shields, the Credit Doubler and the Fleet Bundle are one-time purchases and are restored for free with the same Apple or Google account.

How do I back up or move my progress?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore, choose Google Drive, iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or OneDrive, pick your account and tap Back Up Now, or turn on daily, weekly or monthly automatic backups. On a new device, use Restore from Backup. Backups are compressed and encrypted and are stored in your own cloud account.

Are there ads?

No. Astravane has no advertising of any kind.

A purchase did not arrive

Restart the game while connected to the internet; pending purchases are delivered automatically. If it still does not appear, use Restore Purchases or contact us.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Refunds can be requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or from Google Play in your order history.
