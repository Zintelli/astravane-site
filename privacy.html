ASTRAVANE

Privacy Policy · Effective 19 November 2026

Support · Privacy Policy

Summary

Astravane does not collect, store or sell your personal data. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is stored on your device, and copies you choose to back up go to your own cloud storage account.

Who we are

Astravane is published by ZINTELLI Ltd, United Kingdom ("we", "us"). Contact: info@zinteli.com.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps your progress (levels, score, credits, upgrades, owned ships and shields, settings) in a database on your device. It never leaves your device unless you turn on a cloud backup.

Cloud backups (optional)

If you choose to back up, the game signs in to the provider you select – Google Drive, Apple iCloud Drive or Microsoft OneDrive – and uploads a compressed, encrypted copy of your game database to a private app folder in your account (Google Drive "app data", iCloud Drive app container, OneDrive "Apps/Astravane"). We do not receive these files or your sign-in details.

To show which account backups go to, the game reads your account email address and name from the provider. This information and the sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. You can remove an account at any time in Settings → Backup & Restore, and revoke access in your Google, Apple or Microsoft account settings. Your use of these services is covered by the privacy policies of Google, Apple and Microsoft.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives a confirmation of each purchase (product and transaction identifiers) so it can deliver the item; these identifiers are kept on your device.

Children

Astravane does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Cloud backups are optional and use the parent's or player's own cloud account.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app deletes the data on your device; you can delete backups from your own cloud storage. Under UK GDPR you may contact us or the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) with any concern.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date.
